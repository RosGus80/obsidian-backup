- **HP:** 800
- **AC:** 21–23
- **Resistance:** Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic
- **Immunity:** Poison, Disease, Exhaustion
- **Damage Resistance to Nonmagical Weapons**
- **Magic Resistance** (advantage on saving throws vs spells)

**Regeneration 40–60 HP/round**, but:
> Doesnt work on round that the daemon takes radiant damage from a blessed weapon OR is inside consecrated ground.

### 3️Aura of Pandemic (Passive)
Creatures within 20–30 ft:
- Take 10–20 necrotic damage at start of turn
- Must make CON save or gain **1 level of Plague Rot**

**Plague Rot (Stacking Condition):**
- Disadvantage on healing received
- At 3 stacks: vulnerability to necrotic
- At 5 stacks: drop to 0 HP

### Flesh That Refuses to Die (Phase Mechanic)
At 0 HP (first time):
Instead of dying:
- Explodes in infectious mist
- Heals to 300 HP
- Grows wings or mutates
- Gains a new ability set

## Multiattack (3–4 attacks)
Each hit:
- 2d12 + 8 slashing
- 4d10 necrotic
- Target must CON save or become diseased

## Legendary Actions (3 per round)
- **Infect** – Force a CON save on a creature within 60 ft
- **Rot Step** – Teleport 40 ft leaving poison cloud
- **Virulent Surge (Costs 2)** – All infected creatures take 5d10 necrotic


## Mythic Action (Phase 2 Only)
**Apocalyptic Bloom (1/day):**
- Massive 60-ft radius disease wave
- Creatures failing save gain 2 levels of exhaustion
